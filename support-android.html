Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Your purchases are automatically restored when you are signed into the same Google account that was used to make the original purchase. If your purchases are not appearing, make sure you are signed into the correct Google account on your device and try restarting the app. Note that consumable purchases (like hint credits) cannot be restored.

How do I reset my game progress?

You can reset your game progress from the Settings menu within the app. Tap "Reset Progress" and confirm your choice. Please note that this action cannot be undone and will reset all level completions, stars, and statistics.

I'm stuck on a level. How can I get help?

Energy Path offers a hint system to help you through difficult levels. You can use hint credits to reveal the solution step by step. Hint credits can be purchased within the app. If you're still stuck, feel free to contact us!

How do I change the app language?

Energy Path follows your device's language setting. To change the language, go to your device's Settings > System > Languages & input and select your preferred language. The app supports 45 languages.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We love hearing from our players! Please email us at contact@yasserhamek.com with details about any bugs you encounter or features you'd like to see. Include your Android version and device model if reporting a bug.

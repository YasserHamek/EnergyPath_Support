Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

To restore your previous purchases, go to the Settings menu in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". This will restore any chapter unlocks or other non-consumable purchases linked to your Apple ID. Note that consumable purchases (like hint credits) cannot be restored.

How do I sync my progress across devices?

Energy Path automatically syncs your progress across all your Apple devices using iCloud. Make sure you're signed into the same iCloud account on all devices and that iCloud is enabled for Energy Path in your device's Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud.

How do I reset my game progress?

You can reset your game progress from the Settings menu within the app. Tap "Reset Progress" and confirm your choice. Please note that this action cannot be undone and will reset all level completions, stars, and statistics.

I'm stuck on a level. How can I get help?

Energy Path offers a hint system to help you through difficult levels. You can use hint credits to reveal the solution step by step. Hint credits can be purchased within the app. If you're still stuck, feel free to contact us!

How do I change the app language?

Energy Path follows your device's language setting. To change the language, go to your device's Settings > General > Language & Region and select your preferred language. The app supports English, Arabic, and French.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We love hearing from our players! Please email us at contact@yasserhamek.com with details about any bugs you encounter or features you'd like to see. Include your device model and iOS version if reporting a bug.
